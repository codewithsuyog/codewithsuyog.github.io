PhD Tracker – Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who uses it

PhD Tracker is a personal script run by its only user, Suyog Andhare, on his own computer, with his own Google account. Nobody else can sign in to it.

What it accesses

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): reads threads labelled "PhD", creates follow-up drafts, and sends a daily summary email to the same account. It never deletes email and never sends mail to anyone else.
  • Google Calendar (calendar.events): adds reminder events to the user's own calendar.
  • Google Drive (drive.file): creates and updates one Google Sheet, "PhD Tracker". It cannot see any other files.

Where data is kept

Data stays in the user's own Google account (Gmail, Calendar, the tracker Sheet) and on the user's own computer (a sign-in token and daily backups of the Sheet). No data is sent to any other server, sold, shared, or used for advertising or for training AI models.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PhD Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
