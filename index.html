PhD Tracker

PhD Tracker is a personal tool used by one person, Suyog Andhare, to keep track of his own PhD application emails. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Once a day it reads the Gmail threads he has labelled "PhD", records their status (sent, replied, meeting, apply via portal, rejected) in a Google Sheet in his own Drive, creates follow-up email drafts for him to review, and adds reminders to his own Google Calendar.
